Event Recap · Mondial de l’Auto 2026

From Paris to Malaysia: UMPSA, PIX Moving & Move Robotic Seal RoboBus Partnership

At the 91st Paris Motor Show, UMPSA exchanged a Memorandum of Agreement with PIX Moving and Move Robotic for the pilot deployment and testing of RoboBus in Malaysia, witnessed by the Ambassador of Malaysia to France.

12–18 Oct 2026 Hall 7.1 · Stand C01, Paris Expo MoA exchanged 15 Oct 2026
About the MoA View photos
The Agreement

A Memorandum of Agreement signed on the world stage

On 15 October 2026, at the PIX Moving stand, the three partners exchanged an MoA to accelerate the development and adoption of smart mobility, especially autonomous vehicles, in Malaysia. The ceremony was linked live with UMPSA in Malaysia.

“Pilot Technology Deployment on Advanced Mobility Platform RoboBus”

  • DateThursday, 15 October 2026
  • VenuePIX Moving stand, Hall 7.1 · C01, Paris Expo Porte de Versailles
  • PartiesUniversiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA) · Move Robotic Sdn. Bhd. · Pixel Intelligence Co., Ltd. (PIX Moving)
  • Exchanged byAssoc. Prof. Ts. Dr. Muhammad Aizzat Zakaria, Director, Centre for Innovation and Technology Transfer, UMPSA · Angelo (Chuan) Yu, CEO, PIX Moving · Mohd Hazeli Rasul, CEO, Move Robotic
  • Witnessed byHis Excellency Datuk Eldeen Husaini Mohd Hashim, Ambassador of Malaysia to France

“The value of this collaboration lies in the capabilities we can develop together.”

Prof. Ts. Dr. Yatimah Alias, Vice-Chancellor, UMPSA · via Bernama
Who Does What

Three partners, one RoboBus pilot

Research, commercialisation and platform: each partner brings a different strength to the pilot deployment in Malaysia.

Data sovereignty: UMPSA and Move Robotic will jointly develop a strategy for managing project data with Malaysia’s interests in mind, building local algorithm capability and technical knowledge.
