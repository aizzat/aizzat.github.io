ARC FTKPM

Autonomous, Robotic and Control Laboratory

Specializes in autonomous systems, path planning, and advanced vehicle dynamics.

The ARC Laboratory is a premier research facility within Faculty of Manufacturing and Mechatronic Engineering Technology (FTKPM), Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA) dedicated to advancing the frontiers of robotics, autonomous navigation, and intelligent control systems. We focus on bridging the gap between theoretical algorithms and real-world robotic deployments.

Autonomous Micro-Transit

PIX Moving Robobus: Coming to Malaysia

A flagship highlight of the joint collaboration between PIX Moving and ARC FTKPM is the upcoming deployment of the Robobus in Malaysia. Designed as an autonomous mobile space built around passenger comfort rather than a driver's seat, the Robobus delivers zero-emission, programmable micro-mobility for university campuses, smart townships, and urban public transit.

UMPSA (ARC FTKPM) leads the deep-tech R&D pipeline: advancing autonomous vehicle algorithms, multi-sensor fusion architectures, localized sensor tuning, tropical monsoon data collection, custom DREBAR firmware for multi-robot coordination and tracking, campus testbed deployment, and specialized expert R&D training for engineers and clients.

  • Autonomous Vehicle Algorithms & Sensor Fusion: Pioneering multi-modal perception pipelines integrating 3D LiDAR, Radar, cameras, and RTK-GNSS with Model Predictive Control and Extended Kalman Filtering.
  • Local Tuning & Tropical Data Collection: Engineering localized sensor algorithms and executing empirical data collection across Malaysian monsoon rain and extreme heat.
  • Custom DREBAR Firmware for Coordination & Tracking: Proprietary firmware for deterministic drive-by-wire control, real-time fleet telemetry tracking, and collision-free multi-robot coordination.
  • Campus Testbed Deployment Lead: Directing living laboratory proving ground operations across UMPSA Pekan and Gambang campuses.
  • Specialised Expert R&D Training: Certified masterclasses for practicing engineers and enterprise clients in ROS 2, drive-by-wire calibration, and autonomous vehicle safety validation.
  • Move Robotic Deployment & Operations: Turnkey Robobus deployment, SLA maintenance, technical servicing, operational fleet management, and commercial business models in Malaysia.

Homegrown Malaysian Technology

DREBAR Autonomous Robotics Firmware Scaling Globally

DREBAR is ARC FTKPM's flagship proprietary robotics firmware—proudly developed at UMPSA ("Made in UMPSA") and officially registered under multiple trademark classes. At the Paris Motor Show 2026, DREBAR's R&D steps onto the global automotive and industrial robotics stage.

Engineered to bridge the gap between low-level motor actuation and high-level ROS 2 navigation, DREBAR empowers autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), delivery units, and drive-by-wire vehicle platforms with deterministic, real-time control.
