ARC FTKPM

Autonomous, Robotic and Control Laboratory

Specializes in autonomous systems, path planning, and advanced vehicle dynamics.

The ARC Laboratory is a premier research facility within Faculty of Manufacturing and Mechatronic Engineering Technology (FTKPM), Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA) dedicated to advancing the frontiers of robotics, autonomous navigation, and intelligent control systems. We focus on bridging the gap between theoretical algorithms and real-world robotic deployments.
