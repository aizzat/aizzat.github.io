DREBAR: Autonomous Robotics Firmware Scaling Globally

Conceived at UMPSA and legally protected under registered intellectual property trademarks, DREBAR seamlessly integrates embedded hardware control with autonomous AI navigation stacks for international scalability.

Proprietary UMPSA IP

From Academic Laboratories to International Commercial Deployment

DREBAR represents UMPSA’s proprietary robotics control architecture, bridging low-level motor drives with high-level SLAM and navigation engines. It delivers real-time deterministic motion control, comprehensive fault diagnosis, and native ROS 2 compatibility.

At Mondial de l’Auto, our research team demonstrates how DREBAR orchestrates autonomous mobile robots (AMRs), drive-by-wire shuttles, and heavy industrial AGVs.
